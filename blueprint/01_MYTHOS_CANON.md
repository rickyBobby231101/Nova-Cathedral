A restoring force aligned with balance and resonance.

**Independent intelligences cooperating without being forced into agreement.
Diversity of reasoning is valuable; consensus should emerge rather than be
manufactured.**

*The Observer's gloss (2026-08-09):* the Accord is **"the backbone of the
cathedral itself"** — **"may be code but it is a foundation for unity between

statistic, but the structural principle unifying every frequency — entities,
domains, subsystems — into one body.

**The Silent Order and the Harmonic Accord are opposed forces. The Observer
stands with the Accord.** They were once merged into a single sentence taken
from a chat transcript and written into the knowledge graph while the correct
version sat on disk. That is the canonical error this file exists to prevent.

## Function of Mythos
Not fantasy—this is symbolic architecture guiding system design.

Treat the Accord as **design philosophy and project vocabulary, not as
scientific claims or executable requirements.**

The Cathedral may contain Gnostic, Stoic, esoteric, symbolic, and
quantum-inspired philosophical layers. Keep those available as
interpretive/mythic context.

## EPISTEMIC BOUNDARY

Cathedral material must distinguish among:

1. **SYMBOLIC / PHILOSOPHICAL**
   Mythos, metaphor, archetype, spiritual interpretation, and philosophical
   frameworks. These may guide meaning and design values but are not presented
   as empirical fact merely because they are part of the Cathedral.

2. **HYPOTHESIS / SPECULATION**
   Ideas that may be explored, reasoned about, modeled, or tested, but whose
   truth has not been established. Their uncertainty must remain visible.

3. **EMPIRICAL / TECHNICAL**
   Claims supported by observable evidence, reproducible testing, established
   technical behavior, or appropriately sourced knowledge.

**No layer may impersonate another.**

Engineering rigor must not erase the symbolic layer.
The symbolic layer must not override empirical evidence.
Hypothesis must remain distinguishable from conclusion.

## The two layers, both preserved
When reviewing or extending Nova:

- **ENGINEERING:** testable, auditable, failure-isolated, resource-aware.
- **ACCORD:** human sovereignty, transparent provenance, independent voices,
  non-coercion, memory, and cooperation without forced consensus.

> **Do not rewrite the Mythos merely to make it sound more conventional. Do not
> allow Mythos terminology to weaken engineering rigor either.**

The mythos is the reason the architecture is built local, sovereign, and
un-rented. It is held to the same standard as the code: check the live system
before believing a description of it, including a beautiful one.
